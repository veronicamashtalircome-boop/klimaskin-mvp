2 · Product overview

A smart film system
for existing windows

KlimaSkin combines advanced smart film, light and temperature sensing, and solar power in a thin, elegant edge-fit kit. It sits on your existing window — no drilling, no external installation, no landlord approval for façade changes.

KlimaSkin automatically adapts to the sun, the weather and your home’s temperature — so you don’t have to.
